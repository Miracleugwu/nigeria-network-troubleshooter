Safety checks

  • Never share your SIM PIN, banking PIN, OTP or password.
  • Do not install an APK just because it claims to boost your network.
  • DNS changes cannot create cellular signal where coverage is poor.
  • For persistent problems, record your location, time and network type before contacting your operator.
